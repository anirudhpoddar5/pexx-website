Textiles That Live With You

Every interior textile at PEXX begins with breathable cotton, softened finishes, and hand block prints designed to bring warmth and quiet character into the home. The new range moves across layered bedding, airy covers, and table settings that feel relaxed yet refined.

From bedcover sets and quilts to dohars, table linen, and coordinated runners with napkins and mats, each piece is styled to feel tactile, real, and deeply livable while carrying the craft value of hand printing.
