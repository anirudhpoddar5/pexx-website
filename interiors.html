Interiors

Softly layered cotton interiors shaped for calmer rooms, gentler colour, and a more considered home.

Rooms, Softened by Craft

Every interior textile at PEXX begins with breathable cotton, softened finishes, and hand block prints designed to settle naturally into the room. The collection moves across bedding, light layers, and table settings with a quiet sense of order and ease.

From bedcover sets and quilts to dohars, table linen, and coordinated runners with napkins and mats, each piece is intended to feel tactile, enduring, and graciously lived with rather than merely displayed.
