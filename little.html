Little

Soft cotton essentials for naps, play, travel, and growing routines.

Made for Gentleness

The Little range is built around softness, safety, and cheerful print stories that still feel calm in a room. Across the new collection, quilts, swaddles, bed sets, and accessories are imagined as everyday companions for sleep, travel, play, and storage.

We use breathable cotton and skin-safe dyes, then bring in playful motifs — suns, animals, florals, bows, and small transport prints — so each piece feels practical for children while remaining warm, beautiful, and easy to live with.

Why Little by PEXX

Skin-Safe Dyes

All prints use azo-free, OEKO-TEX aligned dyes. No harsh chemicals, no synthetic finishes — safe from first wash through years of use.

Natural Cotton Only

We use 100% natural cotton — breathable, temperature-regulating, and free from synthetic blends that can irritate sensitive skin.

Designed to Last

Colours are washed and set for longevity. The Little range is built to survive the machine, the mud, and the next hand-me-down.
