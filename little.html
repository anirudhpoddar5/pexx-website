Little

Gentle textiles for little lives and growing moments.

Made for Gentleness

The Little range is built around one principle: what touches a child's skin should be as safe as it is beautiful. We use 100% natural cotton, washed to a gentle softness, and printed only with azo-free, skin-safe dyes.

Motifs are drawn from nature — animals, botanicals, simple geometrics — scaled for small spaces and young imaginations. Each piece is designed to be long-lasting, so it can be passed on as well as used up.

Why Little by PEXX

Skin-Safe Dyes

All prints use azo-free, OEKO-TEX aligned dyes. No harsh chemicals, no synthetic finishes — safe from first wash through years of use.

Natural Cotton Only

We use 100% natural cotton — breathable, temperature-regulating, and free from synthetic blends that can irritate sensitive skin.

Designed to Last

Colours are washed and set for longevity. The Little range is built to survive the machine, the mud, and the next hand-me-down.
