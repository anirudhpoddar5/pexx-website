Print Workshops

Immersive sessions inside our printing rooms, where guests work directly with blocks, colour, cloth, and the rhythm of real production.

An Intimate Craft Experience

A PEXX workshop is not a demonstration — it is a hands-on session. You will mix colours, select and ink blocks, print onto fabric, and take home what you make.

Sessions take place inside our production facility, so you see the full process: from raw cloth and block carving to printing, washing, and drying. The workshop is structured around that production rhythm, not a studio simulation of it.

No prior experience is needed. Sessions are available in English and Hindi. Groups of all backgrounds — design students, travelling creatives, heritage tourists, and corporate teams — have participated.

What the Session Holds

01

Factory Orientation

A short walk through production — fabric prep, block carving, printing stations, and finishing.

02

Colour Selection

Choose from a curated palette of azo-free dyes. Guidance on colour mixing and repeat design.

03

Hands-On Printing

Print your chosen design onto natural cotton fabric using traditional wooden blocks — guided by our artisans.

04

Take It Home

Your printed piece is washed, dried, and packaged for you to take home — a made-by-you keepsake.

Practical Information

Location
Our production facility, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. Full address shared upon booking confirmation.
Language
Sessions conducted in English and Hindi. Other languages may be available for large groups — please enquire.
What to wear
Clothes you don't mind getting ink on. An apron is provided, but block printing can splash — dress accordingly.
What you take home
Your printed fabric piece (approx. 1 metre), washed and packaged. Optional additional pieces can be arranged for a fee.
Advance notice
Individuals and small groups: minimum 5 days. Design visits and corporate groups: minimum 3 weeks.
Availability
Sessions run Monday to Saturday, subject to production schedules. We will confirm dates within 2 working days of your enquiry.
