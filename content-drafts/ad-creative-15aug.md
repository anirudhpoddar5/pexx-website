have the next campaign ready" moment.

## Gap worth closing

The skill's grounding rules ask for customer reviews and ad comments as inputs.
You have neither collected. Ad comments in particular are where objections show
up in customers' own words, and they usually make the best next ads. Pulling the
comments off these four ads before they end would cost nothing and would ground
the next round in something better than my inference.
