# PEXX Instagram journal style — locked 23 Aug 2026

Owner-approved. Do not redesign this per post; fill it in.

## Type
- Display: **Prata** (Google). One weight, no italic. Used for the wordmark,
  the headline and the journal title on the closing slide.
  Rejected: Bodoni Moda (too cold, too runway), Marcellus (too quiet),
  Cormorant Garamond (thin and generic at display size).
- Small caps: **Jost**, 300–500, letterspaced 0.15–0.26em, always uppercase.
- Sizes are set in `ig-journal-slide.html` and were raised once on the owner's
  note that the wordmark and footnote read too small. Do not shrink them again.

## Colour
- Cream `#f3ede3`, soft cream `rgba(243,237,227,.74)`
- Gold `#c9a063` — the accent phrase, the diamond rules, the journal title
- Scrim: warm near-black brown, heaviest at the left, opening to the photo right

## Layout
Full-bleed photo. Wordmark top-left, gold diamond rule, tagline
"SLOW. SOULFUL. HANDMADE." Headline sits low-left, max 13ch, one phrase in gold.
Footnote in small caps at the foot, above a second diamond rule.

## Photos
Warm, close, shallow. Grade every source toward the house brown:
`-modulate 94,90,100 -fill '#3a2416' -colorize 16% -brightness-contrast -3x6`
Never a raw catalogue frame — no turf, no label card, no white studio sweep.

## Copy — how the houses do it
The register is Hermès, not content marketing. What that means in practice:

- **Never instruct.** No "please don't buy", no "you should". State, and stop.
- **No problem → solution.** Naming a problem makes us a vendor solving it.
- **No bullet lists of benefits.** Name one thing, singular.
- **Far fewer words.** Four to eight per headline. The object carries the rest.
- **Time is the subject** — seasons, ten years, inheritance. Not features.
- **Imply, never explain.** "And one for her." lands; "she'll appreciate it
  because she's exhausted" does not.
- One phrase per headline in gold. That phrase is the idea.

Worked example, the approved four-slide arc:
1. Some gifts are opened **once**. / Others are folded away, and taken out again every winter for ten years.
2. Cotton. One metre **square**. / Sixty impressions of a wooden block, pressed by hand in Jaipur.
3. And one **for her**. / The gift nobody thinks to bring.
4. Godh bharai. Namkaran. The first **Diwali**. / Read the full journal → title

## Rejected, for the record
Cream cards with a small photo floating in them, bulleted "what actually gets
used" lists, and blog-voice hooks like "Please don't buy the newborn clothes."
Both read as content marketing in a feed that reads as a house.
