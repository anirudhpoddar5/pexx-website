Some gifts are opened once.

Others are folded away, and taken out again every winter for ten years.

A metre of cotton, carrying sixty impressions of a wooden block, pressed by hand in Jaipur.

Godh bharai. Namkaran. The first Diwali. The first birthday.

And one for her — the gift nobody thinks to bring.
