# Seven copywriting styles — same journal, so they can be compared

One article, written seven ways. Same photos, same layout, same typeface —
only the writing changes, so whatever wins, wins on the writing.

Article: **What to Gift a New Baby in India** (shop.poddarexp.com)

**Bold** = the gold accent phrase. The line beneath is the small-caps footnote.
Slide 4 always carries the journal title.

All seven obey the rules from `.agents/research/luxury-copy-patterns.md`:
describe rather than persuade, specifics instead of adjectives, no instruction,
never justify price.

---

## 1. SPEC — Loro Piana, Dior product copy
*Facts, counts, materials. Cool and unhurried. The object is the argument.*

1. **Sixty impressions** of a wooden block. / ONE METRE OF COTTON. FOUR COLOURS. FOUR DAYS OF DRYING.
2. Muslin, at **fifty grams** a layer. / A REGULAR BEDSHEET IS THREE TIMES THAT.
3. It gets **softer** every wash. / THE ONLY GIFT ON THE LIST THAT IMPROVES WITH USE.
4. Godh bharai. Namkaran. The first **Diwali**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 2. SCENE — Ralph Lauren
*A person, a room, a moment. Warmest of the seven. Closest to how a family firm speaks.*

1. Fourteen people will bring **clothes**. / A NEW BABY IN INDIA IS DRESSED BY COMMITTEE.
2. The baby is out of them in a **month**. / USUALLY WHILE THE HOUSE IS STILL FULL OF VISITORS.
3. A quilt outlives the clothes by **ten years**. / BY THEN IT IS THE ROOF OF A DEN.
4. And one for the **mother**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 3. MANIFESTO — Bottega Veneta, Chanel
*Short absolute statements. Poster copy. No scene, no explanation, high conviction.*

1. Cloth outlives **occasion**. / EVERY GIFT IS EITHER KEPT OR STORED.
2. A size is a **deadline**. / WHAT HAS NO SIZE HAS NO EXPIRY.
3. Machines repeat. Hands **respond**. / SIXTY IMPRESSIONS, NONE IDENTICAL.
4. Made to be **inherited**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 4. QUESTION — your current house style
*Opens on a question the reader is already half-asking. Proven on your feed.*

1. What does a new baby actually **need**? / FOURTEEN PEOPLE ARE ABOUT TO ANSWER THAT WRONGLY.
2. Why does everyone buy **newborn size**? / IT IS THE ONE MONTH SHE ALREADY HAS COVERED.
3. What survives the first **year**? / COTTON WITHOUT A SIZE. AND VERY LITTLE ELSE.
4. And who is buying for the **mother**? / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 5. MUSEUM LABEL — provenance
*Written like a caption beside an object in a collection. Dated, placed, technical.*

1. Cotton muslin. Jaipur. Hand block **printed**. / FOUR BLOCKS, FOUR COLOURS, DRIED BETWEEN EACH PASS.
2. Sanganeri, protected by name since **2010**. / BAGRU FOLLOWED IN 2011.
3. Azo-free dye. Washed before **folding**. / 100 × 100 CM.
4. Given at godh bharai, namkaran, the first **Diwali**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 6. PLAIN — Muji
*No adornment whatsoever. Useful, quiet, almost blunt. Confidence through restraint.*

1. Give something without a **size**. / CLOTHES ARE OUTGROWN IN A MONTH.
2. Two swaddles. One light **quilt**. / THAT IS MOST OF IT.
3. Cotton. Azo-free dye. Softer each **wash**. / NOTHING ELSE ADDED.
4. Works for every occasion in the first **year**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

---

## 7. LETTER — the maker's own voice
*First person, from inside the workshop. Risky, and the hardest to fake — which is why it works if it is true.*

1. We are asked this every **week**. / WHAT DO YOU SEND, WHEN EVERYONE IS SENDING SOMETHING?
2. We stopped suggesting **clothes** years ago. / THEY COME BACK UNWORN MORE OFTEN THAN ANYTHING ELSE.
3. We send the quilt, and something for the **mother**. / IT IS WHAT WE SEND IN OUR OWN FAMILY.
4. Godh bharai. Namkaran. The first **Diwali**. / → *What to Gift a New Baby in India*

## What each one is betting on

| Style | Bets that people respond to | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Spec** | Proof. Numbers do the persuading. | Cold if the photo is not warm |
| **Scene** | Recognition. "That is my house." | Drifts into blog voice if it gets long |
| **Manifesto** | Conviction. Confidence reads as quality. | Says nothing if the lines are not true |
| **Question** | Curiosity. Already proven on your feed. | Wears out if every post is a question |
| **Museum label** | Authority. Facts, dated and placed. | No warmth at all |
| **Plain** | Trust. No one is being sold to. | Forgettable in a scroll |
| **Letter** | Intimacy. A real person is speaking. | Only works if the founder actually shows up |

## How to test it properly

Run three, not seven. **Scene, Manifesto and Letter** are the most different from
each other — if you can only learn one thing, learn which of those three your
audience answers.

Watch **saves and shares**, not likes. A save means useful. A share means worth
being seen with. Likes measure the photograph, not the writing.

Once one wins, it becomes the house voice and gets applied across all seven live
journals — and into the website copy, which currently has a different voice again.
