Carry

Quilted cotton accessories designed for movement, gifting, and a more considered everyday.

Utility, Softened by Craft

The Carry range brings hand block printing into quilted cotton bags and accessories that feel soft in the hand yet considered in form. The collection moves between floral totes, graphic duffles, compact pouches, and softly finished crossbody styles with a polished, giftable character.

Each piece balances utility with textile detail, making the range especially suited to boutique retail, wellness travel, private gifting, and buyers looking for natural alternatives to synthetic accessories.
