## Privacy Policy
We collect only what we need to run your order — your name, email, phone, and delivery address — to process it, deliver it, and keep you updated. With your consent, we'll also send the occasional note about new prints.

We don't sell your data. Payments are handled securely by our payment partners (like Razorpay) — we never see or store your card details. We share only what's necessary with the services that help us run the shop and deliver your order.

Want to see, update, or delete your information, or stop hearing from us? Just email info@poddarexp.com and we'll sort it out.

## Terms of Service
By ordering from PEXX (run by Poddar Exports), you agree to our prices, shipping, and returns as published.

Our pieces are hand block-printed, so small variations in print and colour are natural — that's the mark of the craft, not a flaw. Colours may look slightly different on your screen.

Prices are in INR and include applicable taxes; you'll get a GST invoice with every order. If there's a genuine pricing error or we suspect fraud, we may cancel an order — and if you've paid, we'll refund you in full.

All our designs and the PEXX name are ours, please don't copy them. Questions? info@poddarexp.com.

## Shipping Policy
Orders ship within 3 working days and usually reach you across India in 5–8 days. You'll get tracking once it's on the way.

Pay by Cash on Delivery, card, UPI, net-banking, or wallet — all work across India.

Sending a gift to India from abroad? Order from anywhere and ship to any Indian address — billed in INR, your bank handles the conversion. We also ship to the US and UK at a flat rate shown at checkout (any customs duties are the recipient's).

Around festivals, order by the date shown on the product or banner to be safe. Timelines are estimates and can shift during peak periods or courier delays.

## Returns & Refunds
If something isn't right, return it within 10 days of delivery. Email info@poddarexp.com (or WhatsApp) with your order number and we'll arrange a pickup where we can. Items should be unused, unwashed, with tags on.

If it's faulty, damaged, or wrong, we cover return shipping. For a change of mind, return shipping is on you. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days (COD orders via bank transfer or store credit).

Happy to exchange for another print or size, subject to stock. Personalised and bulk/custom orders are made to order, so they're non-returnable unless faulty.
