## Post 2 — "Is Muslin Safe for a Newborn's Skin?"
**Intent:** informational, high-anxiety · **Keyword:** is muslin safe for newborns, best fabric for baby skin
**Internal links:** → Swaddles, → Sleep collection

**Opening (AEO answer):**
> Yes — muslin is one of the safest fabrics for a newborn's skin. It's a lightweight, breathable cotton weave that's soft, gentle, and helps regulate temperature, which lowers the risk of overheating. For maximum safety, choose muslin made with azo-free (non-toxic) dyes and wash it before first use.

**Structure:** what muslin is → why it suits baby skin → what to check (azo-free dyes, GSM, pre-washing) → how to care for it. Naturally links to PEXX swaddles as an example of azo-free muslin.
