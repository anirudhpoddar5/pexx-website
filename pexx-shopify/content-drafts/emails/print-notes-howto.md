# Print notes — the per-product line

The field is live: **Print note**, `custom.print_note`, on every product.
Shopify admin → Products → *(any product)* → scroll to Metafields → Print note.

## What it's for

Two sentences about *that* print, pulled automatically into the abandoned-cart and browse-abandonment
emails. Written once per product, reused forever. Without it those emails simply omit the line —
nothing generic is substituted.

## What good looks like

Say something only true of this print. Where the motif came from, what the colours were chosen against,
who it's for, what it does in a room. Avoid adjectives that would fit any product ("beautiful", "premium",
"lovely") — they're the tell that nobody looked at the cloth.

**Shape to aim for:** *what's on it, in what colours, on what ground — then one line on the thinking.*

Two examples, written from the titles only. **These are illustrations, not approved copy — rewrite them
against the actual prints:**

> Submarines, portholes and a small brave whale, printed in indigo and sea-green on white cotton.
> Drawn for a child who wants the light off but the adventure on.

> A family of bears in charcoal and dusty rose, wandering across soft white cotton.
> Quiet enough for a nursery, interesting enough to be stared at.

## What to avoid

- Anything about scarcity or limited runs — that's been cut from the whole suite
- Care instructions — they have their own email
- Price or discount language
- Claims about the fabric that aren't true of that specific piece (you use several cloths, not just one)

38 products is a lot in one sitting. Do them in the order they're likely to be in someone's basket:

1. The 15 quilts (highest value, most abandoned)
2. The 10 toiletry pouch sets
3. The 6 backpacks
