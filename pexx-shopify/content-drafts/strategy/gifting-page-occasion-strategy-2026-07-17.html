Gifting Page: Occasion & Category Strategy

Prepared 17 July 2026 Research: Fable · Critical review: Opus Nothing here is built. This is the discussion draft.
The one thing to read even if nothing else

"Diwali has enough time" undersells it. For handcrafted bulk production, the corporate Diwali window and the November–December wedding-favour window close at almost the same moment — and they're closing now, not in the autumn. Building the two catalogues one after another guarantees the second one misses its window. They need to move in parallel.

Diwali 2026 Sun, 8 Nov
Corporate orders finalize Mid-Aug – Mid-Sep
Nov–Dec wedding favours sourced Aug – Sep (now)
Festive retail edit live by Late Sep
Retail shoppable product grid Catalog · B2B lookbook → bulk-enquiry quote, no cart

Part B

What we think we should do

Skim this section first. The full research behind it follows below.

B1Final proposed structure

Retail

Evergreen — no deadline, mostly re-slicing products you already have

  • New Baby & First Milestones — one hub covering godh bharai/baby shower, namkaran, annaprashan, mundan as sub-tags (not separate pages), plus a "welcome home" gift-set framing.
  • Kids Birthdays — gifts for the child.
  • Party Return Gifts — the ₹100–400 Bulk-tag band. Covers kids parties, godh bharai return favours, milestone birthday/anniversary favours, school/Teacher's Day — one page, many occasions.
Retail

Seasonal — has a real deadline

  • The Festive Edit — Diwali-led, live by late September. Includes "Baby's First Diwali" sets, a Karwa Chauth/Sargi tag, Rakhi (accessories only — never quilts), light Eid/Christmas tagging. No dedicated page per festival.
  • Holiday/travel coordinated sets — small edit, existing SKUs, whenever convenient.
Catalog · B2B

Lookbook → bulk-enquiry, not a shop

  • Weddings, by function: Welcome Hampers · Haldi · Mehendi · Sangeet · Baraat Entry · Return Gifts · Trousseau Quilts · Reception Table Linen · Kids at the Wedding · Bridal Circle.
  • Corporate: Diwali Gifting · Welcome/Onboarding Kits (the evergreen line) · Client Gifting · New-Parent Kits · Events — with a colorway/branding customization story.

Interesting — not now

  • Housewarming / griha pravesh — the future category's natural launch occasion, once table linen goes retail
  • Bath-linen-adjacent occasions
  • B2B hospital/maternity and preschool welcome kits — pilot with one partner, at most
  • Mehendi/event décor textiles — different buyer (decorators), different competence
  • Retirement, Holi — no genuine product fit for this brand

B2Where we'd push back on the plan

Sequencing

Build Corporate Diwali and Wedding in parallel, both live by mid-August — not one after the other. They share a deadline (see callout above), and they share most of their product photography too (potlis, pouches, stoles, table linen), so building them side by side is cheaper than it looks, not more expensive.

After that: the Festive retail edit (deadline: late September) outranks the deadline-free evergreen pages. Holiday sets and the "not now" list come last.

A5Holiday / travel

"Coordinated holiday sets" — small but real: matching backpack + toiletry kit + swaddle-as-travel-blanket per child, all existing SKUs, zero new production. Low effort, low priority.

A6Weddings — the function-by-function catalog

The buyer here is a family or planner sourcing 50–100+ identical pieces per function, 8–10 weeks before the date. The flow ends at the bulk-enquiry form, not Add to Cart.

  1. Welcome Hampers — especially destination weddings
    Fabric hamper bags/totes, drawstring potlis, coordinated pouches
    Strong — block-print potlis are documented as currently trending for exactly this.
  2. Haldi
    Marigold/yellow-palette potlis, guest stoles, printed cotton napkins
    Strong — pastel/yellow color-coordination is the established trend. Woven cotton, not terry towelling.
  3. Mehendi — "mela theme"
    Favour potlis, hand pouches in bright mela palettes
    Strong for favours. Décor textiles (cushions, floor seating) moved to "not now" — that's a decorator's category, different buyer.
  4. Sangeet
    Jewel-tone potli favours, pouch favours
    Fine, as potlis specifically — squarely inside the pouch-sewing line, not structured clutches.
  5. Baraat Entry
    Swagat stoles/dupattas for baraatis; printed safa fabric lengths (fabric supply only)
    Genuinely in-lane — a hemmed printed rectangle, and "barati swagat stoles" are a real documented practice.
  6. Wedding Ceremony / Reception
    Bulk table linen — runners, napkins, mats
    This is the merchant's own stated bulk capability — a natural showcase for it.
  7. Wedding Return Gifts
    Pouch sets, potli + small-textile combos, totes; premium tier: a quilt for close family
    Strong — sourcing timeline is 8–10 weeks pre-wedding.
  8. Trousseau / Family Gifting
    Quilts for the couple's new home
    Strong and authentic — the Jaipuri razai is a classic wedding/bistar gift. Deserves its own catalog page.
  9. Kids at the Wedding ★ the hero idea
    Goodie bags / activity pouches for guest children across a multi-day wedding
    The single most on-brand, defensible, nobody-does-this concept in this document: a kids-textile brand solving "keep the children occupied across a 3-day wedding." Lead the wedding catalog's marketing with this.
  10. Bridesmaids / Close Circle
    Toiletry kits, totes
    Existing retail SKUs, zero new production — easy win.

A7Corporate — expanding "lot of accessories"

India's corporate gifting market is ₹14,000+ crore and growing. Sustainable gifting is the fastest-growing segment (~9.5% CAGR); ~60% of buyers prefer made-in-India; ~80% want eco-friendly; onboarding/engagement kits are ~26% of volume; average per-employee spend has risen from ₹2,500 to ₹4,000. A handmade, azo-free, artisan-made Jaipur brand isn't fighting this tide — it is the tide.

Realistic product range

Pouches and toiletry kits, tote/conference bags, potli hampers, desk textiles, table runners for office festive décor, hamper-wrap fabric. Laptop sleeves: sample-first only — padded construction with device-fit tolerance is a different, QC-heavy product family.

Sub-angles, in priority order

  1. Diwali Corporate Gifting
    The anchor — and the one with a hard deadline.
  2. Employee Onboarding / Welcome Kits
    Tote + pouch + branded tag
    Critical: the evergreen, year-round corporate line. Doesn't die on November 9th — it's what keeps the corporate page alive between festivals.
  3. Client Gifting
    Premium pouch/table-linen sets
  4. New-Parent Employee Gifts
    HR gifting a swaddle/quilt set for an employee's new baby
    A unique overlap between PEXX's own core retail product and a real, growing HR practice — no generic gifting vendor can match this.
  5. Events / Conferences
    Bulk totes

How corporate genuinely differs from retail: MOQ 50–100, custom colorways matched to brand palette, branded tags/packaging, quote-based pricing via the enquiry form, no fixed SKU.

Gate this one honestly

A custom-carved block with a company motif is a real, charming offer — but block printing is a low-resolution medium. Bold, simple motifs carve and print well; fine logo detail, thin type, and gradients will fail. Offer it with a minimum quantity, an extended timeline, and "motif adapted for block printing" framing — not pixel-accurate logo reproduction.

A8Timing — validated, with more urgency than first thought

Diwali 2026 falls on Sunday, 8 November (Dhanteras 6 Nov → Bhai Dooj 10 Nov).

  • Corporate gifting vendors in India close large custom orders by mid-September; volume books up from the first week of September.
  • Handcrafted bulk runs at scale need 60–75 days of production → order finalization by mid-August.
  • Jaipur's monsoon (July–September) slows block-print drying — which moves the effective deadline earlier, not later.

Wedding season: Chaturmas means essentially no North Indian muhurats Aug–Oct 2026 (South Indian and some communities don't follow this strictly). Muhurats reopen with Devuthani Ekadashi in November and the season peaks late Nov–Feb, with a second window Apr–Jun 2027. Favours are sourced 8–10 weeks pre-wedding → November–December wedding families are sourcing in August–September. Right now.

Retail festive isn't deadline-free either: the Sept–Oct festive ramp (Karwa Chauth in October, Dhanteras week) means the Diwali retail edit needs to be live by late September, stock and photography done.

This draft was stress-tested by an independent critical review pass (Opus), which resulted in: downgrading laptop sleeves to sample-first, cutting décor textiles to "not now," gating the custom-block offer, upgrading Rakhi's accessories angle and the kids-at-the-wedding concept, adding Karwa Chauth/Sargi, godh bharai return gifts, Baby's First Diwali, and year-round onboarding kits, and re-sequencing the priorities to parallel B2B builds against a late-September festive retail deadline.
