## FAQ (FAQ schema)

**Can I send gifts to India from the USA / UK / abroad?**
Yes. You pay in your own currency (USD, GBP, AED, CAD) with an international card or PayPal, and we deliver to any address in India. The recipient receives a gift-wrapped parcel with your personal message — no price shown.

**How long does delivery within India take?**
Orders are dispatched within 3 working days and typically delivered across India within 5–8 days. For festivals like Rakhi and Diwali, order by the date shown on the festival page to ensure on-time delivery.

**Are the products safe for babies and children?**
Yes — soft cotton with azo-free, non-toxic dyes, gentle on young skin.

**Can I add a personal message?**
Yes, free of charge. Add your note at checkout and we include it on a card inside the parcel.

**Will the recipient see the price?**
No. Gift orders ship without any invoice or price on the packing slip.

**Which festivals can I send gifts for?**
Rakhi, Diwali, birthdays, new-baby welcomes, and naming ceremonies, among others. Each festival page shows the order-by date for guaranteed delivery.
