How to print

  • Paper: matte or satin, 250–300gsm. Printed side only — the branded back never shows.
  • Chrome → Print: Paper size A4, Margins None, Scale 100% (not "Fit"), Print backgrounds ticked.
  • Cutting: there's a ~6mm white alley between cards. Cut on the two grey hairlines either side of each alley and the strip falls away, leaving cards at exactly 4″ × 2.75″. The frame sits 3.6mm inside the trim, so a 1mm wobble only changes the white margin — it never clips the border.
  • Outer edges: the cards run flush to the sheet, so the four outside edges are the paper's own factory edges — nothing to cut there.
