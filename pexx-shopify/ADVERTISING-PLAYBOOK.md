# PEXX — Advertising Playbook

**What this file is:** the living strategy layer — channel decisions, copy
research, and creative direction. It exists so research doesn't get redone
every session. Companion to [META-ADS-RUNBOOK.md](META-ADS-RUNBOOK.md), which
owns Meta *account mechanics* (IDs, incidents, API quirks) — this file owns
*strategy* (what to say, where to spend, why).

**Category reminder, every time this file is read:** PEXX sells a premium
physical, emotional/gifting purchase (hand block-printed baby/kids quilts,
backpacks, toiletry pouch sets, ₹999–₹1,299, India-only, COD available).
Generic SaaS/B2B growth-marketing advice (lead scoring, MQL/SQL funnels, long
sales cycles) does not transfer here — discard it on sight rather than
adapting it.

Decided 21 Aug 2026, from Meta Ad Library research. Full reasoning lives in

## Backpack/pouch fix: one bundled product, warm traffic only

Decided 21 Aug 2026. FirstCry sells the backpack + pouch set as **one kit** to
someone already searching with intent; PEXX's site sells them as two SKUs on
two collection pages and expects a cold scroller to assemble the idea. Fix:

1. Build **"The Little Traveller Set"** — backpack + matching 3-piece pouch
   set, ~₹1,699 (vs ₹2,198 apart), 2–3 print options max, only where both
   halves are in stock (3 of 16 current SKUs are sold out).
2. Land only on that product's own page.
3. **No cold Meta prospecting for this category — it already failed once
   cleanly.** Spend only on warm/retargeted traffic (recent site visitors,
   ATC-non-purchasers) + Google Shopping, which is closer to FirstCry's
   intent-driven discovery than a cold Instagram interrupt.
4. Watch add-to-cart rate on that PDP alone: target ≥8% (site-wide is
   currently 1%). Under 3% after ~150 sessions → kill it, it's the product/
   price, not the funnel.

## Copy & creative research (Meta Ad Library, India, live ads read 21 Aug 2026)

**The core finding: nobody in this category advertises price, COD, or
dye-safety as a headline. All three are open lanes for PEXX specifically.**

Real competitor patterns found (not blog theory — read directly off live
Indian ads):
- **Trust-stack as a closing line on every ad:** "14 day easy returns | Express
  Shipping | COD Available" (Lobha Deepthis). COD stated as a *feature*, not
  buried.
- **Price inside the headline:** "₹999 | Blue Floral Handblocked Cotton
  Sling Bag" (Vastra By Monty, PEXX's closest direct comp on price). Raamaé's
  16-month evergreen ad, by contrast, never states a price — that's the gap
  PEXX's ₹1,299-vs-₹2,620 story fills.
- **Counted social proof:** "trusted by 2 lakh+ customers," "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.9/5)" as
  the headline. Round, specific, numeric.
- **Objection-handling as concrete specs**, not adjectives: "zero shrinkage ·
  3 inch seam allowance · 2 spacious pockets," not "premium quality."
- **Azo-free/dye-safety exists but is almost unused** — only ~21 active India
  ads mention it at all, all buried as bullet #4. PEXX's "safe to chew" is a
  genuinely open lane. **Do not ship this claim without the owner confirming
  it's actually substantiable.**
- **First-person mum voice beats brand voice** (Skergo, SuperBottoms' "Quit
  the Guilt" campaign — 250+ mother WhatsApp ambassadors).
- **Format:** short (2–6 lines) and emoji-bullet-stacked is the norm, not
  long-form — treat any single long-copy example as an outlier, not a
  pattern. Question/anxiety-hook openers work ("Confused what to pack for
  delivery? 😰"). Hinglish works in the hook line ("Raksha Bandhan par apno ko
  do gifts they'll love 💝").
- **Bundles sell on item-count/price math**, not "curated set" language
  ("35 must-have items in one bag").

**Ready-to-adapt lines (Quilt/newborn gifting):**
1. "Baby shower gift, sorted. ₹1,299 — and yes, COD."
2. "Before you gift that ₹2,600 quilt, read this. Same hand-block cotton.
   Half the price." (direct at Raamaé/Kari)
3. "Woh har cheez muh mein daalta hai. That's why our dyes are azo-free."
   *(only if substantiable)*
4. "Everyone gifts clothes she'll outgrow in 3 months. Gift the quilt she'll
   still sleep under at 3 years."
5. "Hand block-printed. Azo-free dyes. Safe when she chews the corner —
   because she will. ₹1,299 · COD · Free shipping above ₹750"

**Ready-to-adapt lines (backpack+pouch travel kit):**
6. "Bag ₹999 + pouch ₹1,199 = ₹2,198. Together: ₹1,699." (the maths is the
   hook)
7. "Packing for a 3-day trip with a toddler? One backpack. One pouch. Done."
8. "The set that sells everywhere else — now direct from us, cheaper."
   *(per owner: this category sells consistently well across marketplaces —
   don't hedge this claim, just don't over-specify unverified numbers)*

**Format takeaway:** test cheap native-feeling statics in volume alongside
1-2 videos, not one expensive polished video. Modern Meta (Andromeda-era)
rewards creative volume + broad targeting over interest-stacking — see the
`ads` skill's Meta playbook for the full reasoning. This sits in tension with
"every sustained competitor ad is video" — resolve by testing both, not
picking one dogmatically.

**Unverified / flagged:**
- Ad-Library longevity is a profitability *proxy*, not measured ROAS.
- "Mother-in-law approval" gifting psychology is well documented generally
  but not found in any live baby-category ad — untested here, don't assume.
- iTokri's long-form copy (1200 words) could not be confirmed as a broader
  working pattern — treat as an outlier.

## BUILT 21 Aug 2026 — 4 product carousels, all PAUSED

Campaign `PEXX_Sales_ProductTest_Aug2026` (`120254300323080140`). Built by
`scripts/build_carousels.py` — re-runnable, and it hard-fails if any card's
product is out of stock, so it can't silently advertise something unbuyable.

**Structure: 4 separate ad sets, one per product line.** Deliberate, so each
product gets a discrete budget and its own targeting — a clean per-product
read. `is_adset_budget_sharing_enabled=false` for the same reason (sharing
moves 20% between ad sets and would blur the comparison).

**Each ad is a 5-card carousel. Card 1 = the print up close (print is the
hero); cards 2-5 each show a DIFFERENT USE.** Every card deep-links to its own
product page with UTMs — this finally fixes the collection-page leak that has
been the standing top suspect since 17 Aug.

**Targeting differs per product by real Meta `family_statuses` segments
matched to the child age the product actually suits** — not copy-paste, and
not interest-stacking (which now hurts):

| Ad set | Age | Parent segment (child's age) | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PT_quilt_parents_27to42` | 27-42 W | 0-12m + 1-2 + 3-5 | ₹300 |
| `PT_swaddle_parents_25to38` | 25-38 W | 0-12m only | ₹150 |
| `PT_pouch_parents_28to45` | 28-45 W | 1-2 + 3-5 + 6-8 | ₹150 |
| `PT_backpack_parents_28to45` | 28-45 W | 3-5 + 6-8 | ₹150 |

All: women, India, ADD_TO_CART optimisation, IG feed/story/reels/explore +
FB feed, **no Audience Network**, `advantage_audience: 0` (expansion would
override the age segments that are the entire point — and on 18 Aug it spent
on 18-24 and 55+ for nothing).

**Copy leads with the open lanes** found in the Ad Library research: price
stated outright, COD as a feature line, azo-free dyes said plainly. Framing is
everyday-parent, not gifting — see [[project_positioning_parents_not_gifts]].

**Known limitation, stated honestly:** at ₹150/day the three small ad sets
will not exit Meta's learning phase. Judge them on CTR and add-to-cart *rate*
for the first ~10 days, not purchase count. That is the real cost of a 4-way
split and it is worth paying to get the per-product read.

**Two quilt lines, not size variants** (this was got wrong once): Baby Quilt
100x100 = **₹999** (8 prints), Kids Quilt 110x150 = **₹1,299** (6 in stock).
That is a repeat ladder inside one category — ₹999 at 0-2, ₹1,299 at 3-6.

## Channels beyond Meta/Google (researched 21 Aug 2026)

Ranked by cash-efficiency for this budget, verified against real published
rates, not inference:

1. **WhatsApp warm broadcast — do this first.** India marketing-template
   rate is ~₹1.02/message incl. GST. Broadcasting to ~1,000 past
   buyers/opt-ins costs ~₹1,000 — the cheapest high-intent reach available,
   and PEXX already owns the number + worker infra. Requires real opt-in and
   an opt-out button on every template (STOP handling already exists in the
   worker) — **never** cold/scraped numbers, that gets the number
   quality-rated down.
2. **Nano-influencer barter seeding, 10–15 parenting creators (1–10k
   followers).** Cash cost ≈ product only (~₹4–6k in COGS across 10
   seedings) vs. the same money buying 6–10k cold Meta impressions — and it
   generates reusable UGC as a side effect. Expect ~50% of asks to want cash
   instead of barter at this point in the market.
3. **Amazon India Sponsored Products, ~₹500/day, on the backpack+pouch
   bundle specifically.** Fits the budget shape (practical floor ~₹500/day,
   D2C CPC ₹12–35) and Amazon's *searched* gifting query behaviour is much
   closer to what already converts on FirstCry than a cold Meta interrupt.
4. **One deliberate ₹20,000+GST month on FirstCry ADBees**, once cashflow
   allows a lump sum (self-serve minimum is ₹20k/campaign — not a trickle
   channel). This is the highest-conviction paid channel since FirstCry is
   already the proven-converting marketplace for this exact product.
5. **Organic, always, in parallel — near-zero cash cost:** Reels of the
   actual block-printing process (unusually watchable), organic Pinterest
   pinning (pins compound for years, India traffic growing), long-tail SEO
   for gifting search terms.

**Ruled out:** Pinterest *paid* ads — no India self-serve account exists;
sold only through resold partners with minimums far above this budget.
Flipkart PLA — cheap but skews price-led, poor fit for a premium handmade
product; revisit only if the cheaper channels run out of room.

**Structural catch on any marketplace channel:** it grows marketplace
revenue, not the PEXX site directly. Use insert cards in marketplace parcels
to migrate buyers over, if repeat-direct revenue matters more than volume.
