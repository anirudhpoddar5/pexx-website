Wear

Soft layers designed for comfort and calm.

Clothing as Craft

The Wear range applies the same principles as our home textiles to garments: natural fabric, deliberate pattern placement, and hand-applied print. Each piece is cut and sewn at our integrated facility, allowing us to control quality across cloth, print, and stitch.

Silhouettes are relaxed — designed for ease, layering, and cross-cultural appeal. We work with hospitality buyers, independent boutiques, and fashion-adjacent export clients who want something that carries genuine craft value.
