Wear

Hand block printed cotton garments cut for softness, movement, and a quieter kind of elegance.

Softness as Craft

The Wear range brings hand block printing into an easy wardrobe of dresses, PJ sets, kaftans, kimonos, shirts, and wraps. Across the collection, the mood is fluid, breathable, and quietly expressive, with silhouettes that move naturally between home, travel, and everyday dressing.

Natural cotton, softened finishes, and restrained colour stories keep each piece relaxed on the body, while ethical stitching and small-batch print work preserve the intimacy of handmade cloth.
