## Production and ordering (verified 2026-08-22)

### Ours, from the owner (2026-08-22) — use these, not the benchmarks below

- **Orders take 45 to 60 days.** The range is real and moves with three things:
  the **quantity**, the **fabric type**, and the **weather** (printed cloth is
  washed and dried in the open, so a wet week costs days nobody can buy back).
- **New blocks take 10 to 14 days**, and that sits **inside** the 45–60, at the
  front — it is not added on top. Earlier the owner put it as "deliveries start
  within 45 days even when new blocks have to be cut", which is the same shape.
  Never publish 45–60 *plus* 10–14; that over-quotes by a fortnight.
- **Do not publish a week-by-week production schedule.** Give the number and
  what moves it, not a breakdown of every stage.
- **MOQ: do not publish a number.** The position is deliberate — we value
  relationships built over time, so we stay flexible on a first order while both
  sides learn how the other works. Write it that way; never invent a figure.

**The table below is industry benchmark data, not Poddar's measured numbers.** They came from
sourcing and gifting guides, not from our own records. Before any of them go in
a buyer-facing post, check them against what we actually do — a lead time we
publish is a promise.

## Wash and care (verified 2026-08-22)

First wash separately in cold water; slight bleed from natural dyes is normal.
Cold or lukewarm, mild detergent, gentle cycle. Turn inside out. No bleach, no
tumble dry, no dry cleaning. Dry in shade. Colours mellow slightly over the
first washes — that is the material, not a defect. Cotton and mulmul soften with
every wash.

## Techniques we do NOT use

- **Dabu.** Nothing we make is dabu (the mud-resist method). Owner-confirmed
  26 Aug 2026. Do not describe it as ours, and do not include it in a
  "styles of block printing" comparison as though it were part of the range.

## Claims we do not make

- No GOTS or OEKO-TEX certification. Both are being pursued; neither is held.
  Say "azo-free dyes", "organic-cotton standard".
- No fabricated artisan names, no invented factory audit results, no fake
  customer numbers.
- No discount-led framing on the retail side — the brand uses scarcity and
  limited runs instead.
